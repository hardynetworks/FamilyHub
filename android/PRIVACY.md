# FamilyHub for Android: privacy policy

_Last updated: September 30, 2026_

FamilyHub is a self-hosted family organizer. The Android app connects **only to the FamilyHub server that you (or your family) run and choose** on first launch.

- **What the app stores on your phone:**
  - the address of your FamilyHub server
  - your sign-in cookie for it
  - the notification settings your server provides
- **Your family's data:** calendar events, lists, chores, meals, photos and camera images are loaded from your own server and are not sent anywhere else by the app.
- **Push notifications:** if your server is set up for them, the app registers with Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, and your server stores the resulting device token. Notification content is sent by your server through Firebase to your phone. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **The developers of FamilyHub collect nothing.** The app has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
- **Deleting your data:** sign out, or uninstall the app, and remove your account on your FamilyHub server. Your server's administrator (usually you) controls everything stored there.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/hardynetworks/FamilyHub/issues
